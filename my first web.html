About Zidana Data Consultancy

Evidence-Driven Analytical Excellence

Zidana Data Consultancy is a regional statistics, M&E, and artificial intelligence consultancy firm operating across Southern Africa. We bridge the gap between technical data engineering and executive policy decision-making. Our consultants bring over 15 years of experience in managing large-scale population surveys, spatial epidemiology, clinical biostatistics, financial forecasting, and machine learning deployments.
